Description

Number 6 The Willows is a stunning, 4-bed semi-detached home situated in the highly sought-after, Ballinahinch Wood in the delightful village of Ashford, Co. Wicklow.

Measuring c. 121 Sq M overall No. 6 overlooks a large green area to the front and includes a bright and welcoming entrance hall, cosy and spacious living room and a beautifully appointed, open plan kitchen/ dining room which overlooks the generous back garden. The ground floor also includes a spacious guest wc. There are three generously appointed bedrooms one currently in use as a home office and a wonderful master bedroom with en-suite and a family bathroom. The property has been finished to the highest possible standards.

The houses at Ballinahinch Wood are A2 rated and have been designed to achieve the very best levels of of energy efficiency. Heating is via an innovative Panasonic air to water heat pump with zoned controls. The homes feature high levels of insulation throughout.

The front garden includes spacious cobble locked drive with ample parking for two cars. The west facing back garden which can be accessed via the gated side entrance or french doors from the kitchen and includes a large patio area and a raised lawn.

The Willows is an exclusive and low-density part of the Ballinahinch Wood development with just 26 homes and with access to private and beautifully landscaped green areas and within easy walking distance of Ashford village and all its wonderful amenities.

Ashford is a thriving village located approximately 40 km south of Dublin and 7 km north of Wicklow town. The area is famous for the surrounding countryside with award winning beaches & forest walks just a stones throw away. There are excellent schools, shops, pubs and restaurants all in the immediate vicinity including Avoca at the world famous Mount Usher Gardens. Ashford has become a haven for the commuter and one of the most sought after areas of Co Wicklow. Dart services are available from Greystones and the M11 roadway has brought Dublin even closer with the M50 and Dundrum shopping centre less than 25 minutes away.
